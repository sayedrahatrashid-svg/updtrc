REYCO GROUP — IMAGE FOLDERS
===========================

Drop image files into the folder for the page they belong to.
Use lowercase names, no spaces (use hyphens), e.g. who-we-are.jpg, line-1.jpg.
Prefer .jpg for photos, .svg/.png for logos & graphics.
Aim for ~1600px wide and under ~400 KB each so the site stays fast.

After you add files, tell me which folder(s) you filled and I'll place
them on the page and rebuild. Naming a file after its slot (hero, who-we-are,
gallery-1...) lets me wire them up with little or no description from you.
